Junjie Huang is a Ph.D candidate at the [COMPASS Lab](https://compass.sustech.edu.cn/), Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech), advised by Prof. [Fengwei Zhang](https://fengweiz.github.io/). His research focuses on **confidential computing** and **system security**, with particular interests in accelerator architecture and AI/LLM security.

He has published papers at top-tier venues including **NDSS'26** (CCF-A), **MICRO'25** (CCF-A), and **ICCD'25**. Previously, he received his M.E. and B.E. from Xiangtan University.
